# Lorenzo Pazzaglia — what the catalogue is missing

Assembled 2026-08-22 from the brand's retailers (Venba, Maxaroma) and its own
product pages. Fragrantica's designer index blocks fetching, so this is the
**currently-retailed lineup**, not the full ~37-release back catalogue.

Everything in the house is **extrait de parfum**, typically 50ml and 100ml —
which is why the four already listed sit at 1,300–1,450 with 5ml/10ml decants.
New entries should follow that shape.
